# Freelancer-Data-Cleaning-Interactive-Dashboard

## Objective: 
To transform raw sales data into a clean, structured dataset and analyse customer purchasing behaviour, demographic trends, and sales patterns using Microsoft Excel.

## Overview:
This project focuses on cleaning and analysing raw sales data to make it usable for reporting. 
The raw dataset contained duplicates and inconsistent formatting.

## Project Workflow

## 1. Data Cleaning Process:
- Removed duplicate records
- Standardised formats (e.g., text)
- Cleaned inconsistent numbers and text data

## 2. Analysis:
- Used Pivot Tables to summarize sales data
- Calculated average income per purchase
- Analysed freelancer activity status (active vs inactive)
- Analysed distribution by country and language

## 3. Dashboard: 
- Built an interactive Excel dashboard using charts and PivotTables
- Visualised key insights such as average rating, years of experience, hourly rate,
user counts by country and language and activity status. 

## Key Questions 
## 1. Which freelancer field has the highest and lowest average rating?
Freelancer field that has the highest average rating were DevOps (3.1/5.0 rating)
Meanwhile AI had the lowest rating (1.9/5.0 rating)

## 2. Which countries contribute the most freelancers to the marketplace?
South Korea with 68 people, followed by Canada with 65 people and Germany with 52 people

## 3. Which languages are most commonly spoken by freelancers?
English (215 people)
Spanish (142 people)
Korean (68 people)

## 4. Is there a relationship between years of experience and hourly rate?
- There were not much difference from the average years of experience the freelancer had with their hourly salary.

## 5. How freelancers are active versus inactive?
- 446 are active, 465 are inactive

## Key Findings 
- Freelancer ratings varied considerably by field. DevOps had the highest average rating at 3.1/5.0, while AI had the lowest at 1.9/5.0, indicating differences in freelancer ratings across fields.
- Freelancers were concentrated in a few countries. South Korea had the largest number of freelancers (68), followed by Canada (65) and Germany (52).
- English was the most commonly spoken language. English was spoken by 215 freelancers, followed by Spanish (142) and Korean (68), suggesting that English is the dominant language on the marketplace.
- Experience did not show a strong difference in average hourly rates. The average hourly rate remained relatively similar across different experience levels, suggesting that years of experience alone may not strongly determine hourly earnings in this dataset.
- Freelancer activity was almost evenly divided. There were 446 active freelancers and 465 inactive freelancers, with inactive freelancers slightly outnumbering active freelancers. This suggests that maintaining freelancer engagement may be an area worth examining further.
